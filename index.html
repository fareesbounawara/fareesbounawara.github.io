Who am I?

INT. Who am I? — ONE ACTOR. EVERY ROLE.

I'm Farees — I make films & videos people remember.

Making movies and videos isn't just something I do; it's a passion that drives me. From directing and cinematography to storytelling, editing, and writing, I love every step of bringing an idea to life through visuals.

Whether it's a cinematic film, music video, social media content, or a simple idea turned into something memorable, I'm always looking for new ways to tell stories and create visuals that connect.

If you don't like the shoot… CUUTTT! & do it again.
