Behind The Vision

Farees Bounawara born in Tunisia on August 29, 2000. Movies or videos isn't just something I create; it's a passion that drives me. From directing and cinematography to storytelling, editing, and writing, I love every step of bringing an idea to life through visuals.

Whether it's a cinematic film, music video, social media content, or a simple idea turned into something memorable, I'm always looking for new ways to tell stories and create visuals that connect.
